### ⚠️ Where the cycle actually is — read this first

**The Canadian campus consulting cycle is structurally closed to a 2026 graduate right now.** Every open campus window targets the **Class of 2027**:

- **McKinsey Canada** Business Analyst — window open July 1 to Aug 11, 2026, but recruiting the Class of 2027
- **BCG** undergraduate — June–October window, same "next year's grads" model
- **KPMG Canada** — all 18 current student postings are tagged Fall/Summer/Winter **2027**
- **Kearney** Business Analyst — explicitly restricted to students graduating in **2027**

The one campus role that did match a 2026 graduation — **Monitor Deloitte's QuickStart Strategy & Business Design, Montreal** — closed **January 16, 2026**.

**Your open lane is the rolling experienced-hire track**, which doesn't run on an academic calendar. Both roles below sit there. Don't wait on campus postings that structurally exclude you.

## 🔄 Maintenance

Verified rows carry the date they were checked. **Re-verify anything older than ~1 week** — listings this board has tracked have gone dead in as little as four days. Roughly **20% of listings expire within a week**.

**For campus and new-grad postings, always re-read the stated graduation year.** Firms roll these to the next class without notice and the replacement looks nearly identical. This is the single most common way a listing here goes stale while still appearing live.

**Never trust an aggregator.** Indeed, Glassdoor, Built In, and Communitech's Work In Tech board have all served postings that were closed or absent from the employer's own ATS. Communitech's board in particular mirrors roles that no longer appear in the primary system.

**Scrapeability, for future refreshes:**
- *Reliable:* Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok, Cohere, Ashby boards, Greenhouse, Getro/Consider boards, Deloitte, EY
- *Awkward or blocked:* Microsoft (undocumented search URL scheme, 404s on constructed queries — needs interactive navigation), NVIDIA and other Workday instances (need non-guessable facet IDs), Salesforce, Databricks, Adobe

**Known gap:** Microsoft Canada could not be verified this round. LinkedIn cross-checks suggest live Toronto Business Program Manager and Strategy & Operations roles exist — treat as unknown rather than absent.
